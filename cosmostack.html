CosmoStack

Catch. Stack. Clear. Restore a galaxy.

CosmoStack is a fast arcade puzzle set among the stars. Colored tiles tumble down a glowing conveyor: catch them on your platform, drop them into the well and line up three of a kind to make them vanish. Every cleared line earns coins, and every coin helps bring a ruined planet back to life.

Easy to learn in a minute, hard to put down.

App Store Google Play

How it plays

Catch

Swipe to slide your catcher under the falling tiles.

Stack

Hold up to five tiles on the platform, then swipe down to drop each one where it counts.

Clear

Line up three or more of the same color in a row, a column or a diagonal. Two lines crossing at one tile make a rare cross.

Chain

One drop can set off a cascade of clears. Chain them for big scores.

Stay alive

Don't let a column reach the top. A column about to overflow glows red, so you always get a warning.

A galaxy to rebuild

  • Missions with their own goals: reach a score, clear vertical, horizontal, diagonal or cross lines, or clear a number of tiles.
  • Worlds to restore: travel across the galaxy, from the green plains of Verdant Prime to the shining crust of Crystallon and beyond.
  • Buildings that matter: build and upgrade reactors, factories, labs, prism towers and more. Each one gives you a bonus, such as extra coins or cheaper fuel refills.
  • Watch planets come alive: barren land turns green, seas clear and clouds drift back as you play. Finish every mission and raise every building to restore the planet, and a celebration sweeps across the globe.
  • Keep playing: once a planet's missions are done, replay them to keep earning coins for its buildings.

Fuel, coins and crystals

⚡

Fuel powers every game. Your tank refills over time.

🪙

Coins come from your matches and missions, and build up your planets.

💎

Crystals are rare: they drop from matches now and then, and you earn more for upgrading buildings and restoring planets. Spend them to continue a game or to top up your fuel.

Play your way

  • Separate volume controls for music and sounds.
  • Haptics and screen effects (shake and flashes) can each be turned off.

  • Available in English, French, German, Italian, Portuguese, Russian, Spanish and Ukrainian.
