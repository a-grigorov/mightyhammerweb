Paint World Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 12, 2026 · For CosmoStack, see the CosmoStack privacy policy.

This Privacy Policy applies to Paint World, published by MightyHammer and available on Google Play and the Apple App Store.

1. Introduction

MightyHammer ("we", "us", or "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and share information when you use our mobile games and visit our website at mightyhammergames.com.

By using our apps, you agree to the collection and use of information as described in this policy.

2. Information We Collect

Our games are designed to be simple and privacy-friendly. We do not require you to create an account or provide personal information to play. However, certain data may be collected automatically through third-party SDKs:

  • Device information – device type, operating system version, unique device identifiers (such as IDFA or Android Advertising ID)
  • Usage data – gameplay events, session duration, in-app interactions, and crash reports
  • Advertising data – ad impressions, clicks, and related interaction data collected by ad network partners
  • IP address – used to determine approximate geographic region (country/region level)

We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information such as your name, email address, or phone number.

3. How We Use Your Information

The data collected is used for the following purposes:

  • Delivering and displaying relevant advertisements within our games
  • Analyzing app performance and improving game quality
  • Diagnosing technical problems and crashes
  • Understanding aggregate usage patterns to improve user experience
  • Complying with legal obligations

4. Third-Party Advertising Partners

Our apps use third-party advertising SDKs to display ads. These partners may collect and process data in accordance with their own privacy policies. Our current advertising partners are:

  • Google AdMob – Privacy Policy
  • IronSource (Unity LevelPlay) – Privacy Policy
  • Unity Ads – Privacy Policy

These partners may use advertising identifiers (IDFA/GAID), cookies, and similar tracking technologies to deliver personalized or contextual ads. You can opt out of personalized advertising through your device settings (iOS: Settings → Privacy → Tracking; Android: Settings → Google → Ads).

5. Children's Privacy

Paint World is rated 3+ and is designed to be appropriate for all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or the applicable age in your jurisdiction). Advertising shown in our apps complies with applicable children's advertising regulations. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately.

6. Data Retention

We retain aggregated analytics data for up to 24 months. Advertising data is retained by our third-party partners per their own policies. We do not store personal data on our own servers.

7. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights regarding your personal data, including:

  • The right to access information collected about you
  • The right to request deletion of your data
  • The right to opt out of targeted advertising
  • The right to withdraw consent at any time

For GDPR (EU/EEA) or CCPA (California) requests, please contact us using the details below. We will respond within 30 days.

8. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information. However, no method of electronic transmission or storage is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security of your data.

9. Links to Third-Party Services

Our apps and website may contain links to third-party services (Google Play, App Store). We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those services and encourage you to review their policies.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of our apps after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
