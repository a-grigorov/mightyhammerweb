CosmoStack Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 12, 2026 · For Paint World, see the Paint World privacy policy.

This policy explains what information is collected when you play CosmoStack ("the game"), why, and what choices you have. The game is published by MightyHammer ("we", "us").

Contact: mightyhammergames@gmail.com

In short

  • The game has no accounts. We never ask for your name, email address, phone number or contacts.
  • Your progress and settings are saved on your device.
  • The game uses analytics and advertising services from Google, Unity and Meta. They receive technical and usage data from your device, described below.
  • Where the law requires it, the game asks for your consent first, and you can change your choice at any time.

Information stored on your device

The game saves your progress, in-game currencies and settings (sound, music, language, haptics) in its own storage on your device. This information is not sent to us. Deleting the game deletes it.

Information collected through analytics

To understand how the game is played and to improve it, the game uses Google Analytics for Firebase and Unity Analytics. With your consent where required, these services receive:

  • gameplay events: missions started and finished, scores, in-game currency earned and spent, buildings upgraded, tutorial steps seen, and rewarded ads offered or watched, including the estimated revenue of an ad;
  • technical information: device model, operating system version, app version, language, and country or region, which is estimated from your IP address;
  • an identifier for this installation of the game, created by the analytics service.

This information does not include your name or contact details, and we do not use it to identify you personally.

Information collected for advertising

The game shows rewarded video ads, which play only when you choose to watch one in exchange for an in-game reward. Ads are delivered through Unity LevelPlay (ironSource), Google AdMob and Unity Ads. These partners may collect:

  • your device's advertising identifier: the IDFA on iOS, only if you allow tracking, and the advertising ID on Android;
  • your IP address, which also shows your approximate location;
  • technical information about your device and the game version;
  • how you interact with ads, such as whether an ad was shown, watched or tapped;
  • diagnostic and performance data.

They use this information to deliver and measure ads, to limit how often you see the same ad, to prevent fraud and, if you agree, to show ads that are more relevant to you.

The game also includes the Meta (Facebook) SDK. With your consent it sends app events, such as the game being installed or opened, together with your advertising identifier. This is used to measure how well ads for the game perform on Meta's services.

What we do not collect

We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, precise location or payment information. The game has no purchases with real money.

Your choices

  • Consent message. In regions where consent is required, including the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the game asks for your choices when you first open it. Analytics stays off until you decide. You can change your choices at any time in the game under Settings → Privacy settings.
  • Tracking on iOS. iOS asks whether the game may track you. You can change this at any time in the iOS Settings app under Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Advertising ID on Android. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in the Android Settings app under Privacy → Ads.

If you decline, the game still works. Ads may still be shown, but they are not personalised.

Who receives the information

We do not sell your personal information for money. The services below receive the information described in this policy and handle it under their own privacy policies:

  • Google (Firebase and AdMob): https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • Unity (LevelPlay, Unity Ads and Unity Analytics): https://unity.com/legal/privacy-policy
  • Meta: https://www.facebook.com/privacy/policy

Some privacy laws in the United States treat sharing an advertising identifier for personalised advertising as a "sale" or "sharing" of personal information. You can opt out with the choices above.

These services may process information in countries other than your own, including the United States. They use legal safeguards for such transfers, such as standard contractual clauses.

Legal bases

Where the General Data Protection Regulation or similar laws apply, analytics and personalised advertising rely on your consent. Fraud prevention, security and the basic delivery of ads rely on legitimate interests.

How long information is kept

We do not keep this information on servers of our own. The analytics and advertising services keep it for limited periods under their own retention rules and then delete it or make it anonymous.

Children

CosmoStack is made for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the game, contact us and we will help to have it removed.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal information, to object to its use or restrict it, to withdraw consent, and to complain to your local data protection authority. To make a request, write to us at mightyhammergames@gmail.com. Because the game has no accounts, we may need to ask for details that let the services above find your data.

Security

Information sent by the game is encrypted in transit. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The effective date above shows when it last changed. If the changes are significant, we will say so in the game or on its store page.
